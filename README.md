## 🚀 Featured Research Project

### NETRA — India AI Risk Observatory

An independent research initiative connecting AI-related incidents, evidence, harm, law and accountability.

**Research areas:**
- AI incident documentation and evidence provenance
- Claim–evidence relationships
- Timeline and causal reconstruction
- AI involvement and attribution
